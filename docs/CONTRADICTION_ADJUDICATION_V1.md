# Contradiction taxonomy and adjudication v1.0.0

Allowed outcomes are: confirmed logical contradiction, definition mismatch, scope mismatch, temporal or state difference, rival interpretation, premise disagreement, formal countermodel, apparent tension, superseded claim, and unresolved contradiction candidate.

Scanning compares atomic claims and typed relationships. Similarity or differing polarity can create a candidate, but opposite-looking words alone do not establish contradiction. Declared scope and time differences are surfaced before logical adjudication. Every proposal records both atom IDs and reasons, has a null final status, and is labelled as requiring human review. A reviewer evaluates definitions, quantifiers, scopes, times/states, premises, formal models, and supersession history before assigning an outcome. Only a separately governed review receipt may finalize it.
